# World streaming and state ownership

`WorldStreaming` is a scene child explicitly advanced under the existing gameplay pause gate. `WorldLayout` describes immutable geography, `WorldState` stores compact deltas, and `WorldRouting` plans descriptor-only ground approaches. BeachDemo retains player/evolution/input ownership and compatibility adapters while physical buildings have explicit activation lifetimes.

The starting region has114 permanent compact descriptors. Unloaded generated descriptors release their registry slots. Near chunks own individual structure art and collision; distant chunks batch terrain/roads and building silhouettes without population or physics. Collision activation uses creature scale, swept movement, growth queries, projectiles and responder locations. A structure's full footprint is considered during collision queries, with clearance margins across chunk edges.

Default scheduling budget is2ms per frame for queued chunk work. Individual construction operations and safety-critical collision preparation can exceed that budget; actual spikes must be reported. Detail transitions retain the same footprint and height, with full authored art promoted near the creature. Camera-frustum samples include terrain corners and100-unit rooftops; a scale-dependent candidate region covers distant views. A one-chunk-scale relevance margin reduces churn. Fixed low-cost land/ocean/ridges cover the finite world independently of active simulation.

Movement and projectile sweeps prepare collision before querying it. Offscreen responders route through static descriptions without activating their entire approach corridor; only their current physical region is pinned. Ground hull clearance and stalled-arrival retirement prevent unreachable units reserving the cap forever. Aircraft retain horizontal pursuit and airborne altitude.

Version1 JSON at `user://persistent-world-v1.json` stores generator/seed, modified object records, important vehicle state, player statistics/position/mutation history, DEFCON state and pending evacuations. Geometry and temporary civilian/response/projectile nodes are never serialized. Destruction records include claimed reward state. Autosave runs every10seconds, on completed demolition and application pause; evolution transactions defer saving until stable. A temporary validated file replaces the primary, retaining a previous-good backup. Invalid primary data tries the backup; unsupported future formats and unrecoverable corruption are preserved without overwrite. A version0 delta-format migration is covered by a fixture.

Scripts launched from `scripts/ci` use isolated in-memory worlds unless a suite explicitly selects a unique test save path. Regression scenarios that need distant authored assets request their chunks before inspecting their art or physics. This keeps gameplay assertions while removing the obsolete permanent-residency assumption.
