# Ranked development roadmap

Priority follows controls/stability → integration → Android performance → giant movement → combat feedback → progression/content → polish. This candidate adds no districts or enemy types.

| Rank | Milestone and player value | Dependency / risk | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Accept the integration preview on phone; prevent broken controls, crashes and stalled play. | Requires real Android device and approved build189 comparison. Low code risk; device results currently absent. | Side-by-side install, 15-minute travel/combat, multitouch sprint, background/resume, evolution interruption/reveal, natural feeding and DEBUG EVO at all nine representative sizes. Record device/OS, frame-time/memory/thermal traces and exact build. |
| 2 | Stabilise responder arrival/pathing through industry so combat remains reachable. | Existing side-step heuristic can oscillate; arrival protection may keep a stuck unit in the nine-unit cap. Medium risk. | Long-running approach tests from varied camera headings, APC hull clearance and routes around warehouse corners; preserve offscreen deployment and caps. Extract enemy lifecycle with explicit world/damage interfaces. |
| 3 | Profile and optimise the actual Android world/physics workload. | Depends on #1 measurements. Medium risk for activation changes. | Compare stationary coast, fast district traversal, E120, maximum response, mass demolition and five skins. Introduce region activation or occlusion only if those measurements justify it; prove no collision holes, respawned rewards or travel seams. |
| 4 | Improve giant weight, stride and ground presentation. | Depends on representative foot/clearance tests and phone/art review. Medium/high visual risk. | Review side-view locomotion E34/55/80/120, stance/swing transitions, turns, wall contact and grabbing E22/28. Preserve approved E0–E28 proportions and movement constants. Introduce reach-limited knee/hip articulation or authored gait only with evidence. |
| 5 | Improve damage/readability and combat pacing. | Requires reliable responder approach and phone performance budget. Medium risk. | Clearly legible hit/cover/armour feedback, reliable aiming and fair pressure while evolving; no additional enemy roster. |
| 6 | Tune progression, biomass and purposeful traversal across existing districts. | Requires accepted stable baseline and measured natural play. Medium balance risk. | Record time-to-evolution, diet transitions and meaningful building rewards; avoid content expansion until existing route pacing succeeds. |
| 7 | Optional polish, audio, accessibility and persistence design. | Dependencies above; scope individually. Low to high risk depending on saves/schema. | Prioritise readability/controls before cosmetic effects. Add save migration tests before persistence becomes player-owned production data. |

Architecture work accompanies these milestones: complete enemy ownership, then civilian panic/spawning and vehicle lifecycle; world/camera/HUD afterwards. Continue small tested extractions, with no wholesale rewrite and no automatic merging of the existing draft stack.
