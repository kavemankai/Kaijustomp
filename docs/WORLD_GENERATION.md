# Finite world generation

Work branch: `feature/persistent-streamed-world`, based on integration `00eb61e` (APK217 gameplay source `317743c`). Delivered source `9c4d94e`, APK227; see WORLD_TEST_RESULTS.md for measured results. The full requested extent is enabled without constructing it all at launch. This finite size supports long coastal and deep inland travel while keeping coordinates small enough that origin rebasing was not justified by tests. Travel speed is unchanged; world units are not asserted to be metres.

The world spans X=-10000..10000 and inland Z=-6000, with a continuous sampled shoreline near Z=26. Broad sinusoidal coves begin outside the original 800-unit coastal half-width; terminal headlands turn the shoreline inland in the final 800 units. Low-cost inland and side ridges frame the finite terrain. Coordinates remain Godot world units; no claim that a unit is a real metre or that travel speed has changed.

Generation version1 uses seed79123 by default. A dedicated RNG is seeded from generator version, world seed and integer chunk coordinates; generation never consumes gameplay randomness. Cities have dense cores with commercial and residential rings; ports occupy coastal coves, and military facilities lie within the inland farm/forest geography. Seeded1024x768 regional cells vary surrounding precincts. Seven district families share the established coastal palette and authored building kits, with added windowed towers, farm sheds, vegetation and military fittings. There are no evolution locks. Extent and default seed are editable in `config/world.tres`; only the default dimensions are validated by this release's evidence.

Chunk dimensions are256x256. Floor division handles negative coordinates. Shared roads occupy world-space chunk boundaries; parcel setbacks reserve their width and intersections before placement. The continuous coastal arterial follows the shoreline; the original main road continues inland, and authored cross-street exits connect to the expanded network. Districts overlap in geography rather than forming fixed horizontal bands.

The first114 building descriptors retain their original ordering, positions, dimensions, titles, occupants and reward policy. Generated objects are excluded from those authored reservations. Authored IDs are `authored:000` through `authored:113`; generated IDs are `g1:<chunk-x>:<chunk-z>:<parcel>`, interpreted with the save's generator version and seed. Generated descriptors may be regenerated and their registry slots reused; callers must retain IDs rather than generated indices.

Large structures use the existing size-driven diet/reward policy. Ordinary traffic and civilians are renewable and bounded; authored parked vehicles and building rewards are unique. Additional geography/art variety can extend this generator after the streaming contract is accepted.
